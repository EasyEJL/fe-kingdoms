Full rules and strategy basics
  • You command Blue. Each side takes two orders per turn. Select a territory, then choose an order supplied by Fe. These are two orders TOTAL across your kingdom, not per territory. Selecting tiles is free. End my turn forfeits your remaining orders. After your second order or End my turn, press Run computer turn to let Red act.
  • Move either half your troops (rounded down) or all but one to an orthogonally adjacent territory. Friendly territories combine troops, up to 12. Every territory retains at least one troop.
  • Recruit 2 troops for 3 gold, or add 1 fort level for 2 gold (maximum fort level 2). Both require a supplied territory you own.
  • Defense is troops + fort level + 1 on hills. An attack must exceed defense to conquer; survivors equal attackers minus defense, and captured forts are removed. Otherwise all attackers are lost, while defenders lose max(1, attackers - fort level - hill bonus), with at least one defender surviving.
  • Supply follows a connected path of your territories to your capital. At the start of your turn, gain 2 gold plus 1 for each supplied farm or town, up to 30 gold. Each cut-off territory loses 1 troop, to a minimum of 1.
  • Capture the enemy capital to win immediately. Four consecutive end-turn orders, or completing 120 side turns, ends the match in a draw. Any non-pass order resets the consecutive-pass count.
  • Easy uses greedy one-ply evaluation. Hard uses four-ply beam alpha-beta search. Both use identical rules and resources, with no hidden bonuses. Select difficulty before a new game.
  • Save replay downloads the full deterministic move record. Load replay validates it with Fe. A local checkpoint is saved only after a successful response. Export a replay before replacing a game if you want to keep it.
